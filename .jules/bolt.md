## 2024-05-24 - [O(N^2) Reallocation in C Buffer Growth]
**Learning:** In C, dynamic arrays using a while-loop for capacity growth (e.g., `while (length + needed > capacity) capacity *= 2`) must explicitly update their `capacity` tracker variable after calling `realloc()`. Furthermore, the condition for growing must evaluate against the current *length*, not the total *capacity*. In `http_response_add_data`, using `data_size` instead of `data_length` in the condition, and failing to update `data_size` afterward, resulted in `realloc` being called on *every* append operation, leading to silent O(N^2) behavior and potential buffer overflows.
**Action:** Always verify that struct capacity fields are updated immediately after `realloc`, and double check that growth boundary conditions are calculated using the used length rather than the allocated capacity.
