## 2024-09-19 - IPC Timestamp Optimization & `GetSystemTimeAsFileTime`
**Learning:** In the `airplay2-win` codebase, `xdw_get_sys_time_ms` in `xdw_socket_ipc.c` actually returns microseconds, not milliseconds. It previously calculated the time by converting a `SYSTEMTIME` to a `FILETIME`, splitting it into seconds and microseconds, and then recombining them mathematically. Also, `GetSystemTimeAsFileTime` directly retrieves `FILETIME` and is much more efficient than `GetSystemTime` followed by `SystemTimeToFileTime`.
**Action:** Replace the dual calls and redundant arithmetic split/recombination with a single `GetSystemTimeAsFileTime` call to fetch `FILETIME` directly, compute microseconds, and return the `int64_t` value immediately. Maintain `_WIN32_WCE` fallback for Windows CE compatibility.
